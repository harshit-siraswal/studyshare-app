Still jumping between WhatsApp, Drive links, notices, and ERP? Meet StudyShare: one app for your college study space.

Different college pages are built in. The first fully functional launch is for K. I. E. T. Materials for other colleges will be added soon.

On the K. I. E. T. page, browse notes, P.Y.Qs, videos, syllabus, and downloads by semester, branch, and subject. Search, filter, bookmark, upvote, and open resources fast.

Follow departments like Artificial Intelligence, Events and Activities, and every department that matters to you. You will receive notifications whenever those departments post new notices.

Attendance is built in. Sync K. I. E. T. ERP once, then track overall attendance, subject-wise attendance, low-attendance risk, day-wise records, upcoming classes, and reminders.

AI Chat answers from your college material. AI Studio opens PDFs and YouTube videos with OCR, summaries, quizzes, flashcards, and study chat from the exact content.

Your profile shows contributions, badge stickers, monthly AI credits, and premium status. Premium adds offline PDF downloads, one-year room validity, a premium badge, ten times monthly AI credits, and low-cost top-ups starting at ten rupees.

StudyShare is dark first, with light mode included. The floating study clock and sun-moon theme transition keep it alive while you study.

To download, visit studyshare.in, tap Download, and install the Android APK. StudyShare is currently available on Android.
